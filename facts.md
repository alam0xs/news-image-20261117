  Russia hit bridges in Kyiv the same day.
- "Two killed" is reported by Crimea's Russian-installed governor
  Aksyonov (via Reuters); the poster prints no death number.
- Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC (17 Nov 2026).

### H2 — Iran / Gulf (Middle East)
- Tuesday 17 Nov: IRGC fired drones at a US radar system in Bahrain —
  the 3rd attack in days; Bahrain says all were intercepted, no damage.
- IRGC says two tankers were struck by mines in the Strait of Hormuz;
  UKMTO: tanker Tara Gas attacked twice north of Oman, second strike
  caused a fire on Tuesday, crew abandoned ship.
- US struck a missile site on Qeshm Island (US official via CBS);
  Iran confirms the US struck Qeshm.
- Sources: Reuters, AP, CBS, UKMTO (17 Nov 2026).

### H3 — Trump, Xi, Putin to meet Thursday (Asia)

  sidelines of APEC. First trilateral meeting of the three.
  White House confirmed 13 Nov, Kremlin 14 Nov, China foreign ministry
  confirmed 16 Nov. Agenda: nuclear arms / New START (expired 5 Feb
  2026); Ukraine not on the agenda; no deal expected.

  first stop of a three-day Asia trip; ASEAN–US Special Summit and a
  Quad meeting (US, Japan, India, Australia) — first in two years.
- Sources: Reuters, AP, BusinessWorld (17 Nov 2026).
