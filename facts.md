## HEROES (3)

### H1 — Kerch Bridge (Europe)
- Second Ukrainian strike on the Kerch Bridge overnight 16–17 Nov; the
  road arch/span collapsed into the Kerch Strait; road and rail traffic
  suspended. Kyiv says it is the 7th phase of an SBU operation.
  Russia hit bridges in Kyiv the same day.
- Some sources say the rail arch still stands and rail resumed Tuesday;
  the poster says only "road span falls; traffic halted".
- Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC (17 Nov 2026).

### H2 — Iran / Gulf (Middle East)
- Tuesday 17 Nov: IRGC fired drones at a US radar system in Bahrain —
  the 3rd attack in days; US/CENTCOM says all were intercepted, no damage.
- IRGC says two tankers were struck by mines in the Strait of Hormuz
  (Saturday); UKMTO: tanker Tara Gas attacked twice north of Oman, second
  strike caused a fire on Tuesday, crew abandoned ship.
- US struck a missile site on Qeshm Island (US official via CBS);
  Iran says the US struck Qeshm.
- Sources: Reuters, AP, CBS, UKMTO (17 Nov 2026).

### H3 — Trump, Xi, Putin to meet Thursday (Asia)
- Trump, Xi and Putin to meet THURSDAY 19 Nov in Shenzhen, on the
  sidelines of APEC. First trilateral meeting of the three.
  White House confirmed 13 Nov, Kremlin 14 Nov, China foreign ministry
  confirmed 16 Nov. Agenda: nuclear arms / New START (expired 5 Feb 2026);
  Ukraine not on the agenda; no deal expected.

- Sources: Reuters, AP (17 Nov 2026).
